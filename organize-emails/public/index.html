Email Organizer

A local classifier that learns which emails deserve your attention. Your mail never leaves this machine.

How it works

  1. Connect Gmail with read-only access.
  2. Train it: pull a batch, see what the local model flags, and tell it where it is wrong. The skill file is refined from your corrections.
  3. Use it: classify the last few days and see what needs you today.
